## FAQ for website use
**Does the demo place calls or connect to plant equipment?** No. The public demo is scripted and uses fictional samples; no phone, machine, CMMS, checkout, or actual shop data connection is made.

**Does the AI decide which parts or procedures to use?** No. Generated notes and hypotheses are for qualified people to review using OEM information, safe-work rules, LOTO, and site procedures.

**Is WrenchRelay integrated with my CMMS?** Integration-first is the plan (Fiix read-only sandbox before other vendors); live writeback is not verified, and no integration should be promised.

**What does an Industrial pilot cost?** Defined after a qualified scoped evaluation. No universal Industrial plan or price is established.

**Can I pay for Automotive today?** A published founding price exists, but live payment, tax, webhook fulfillment and phone onboarding have not passed an end-to-end acceptance gate; no payment CTA should be presented on revised marketing until they do.

## SEO / domain rules
- Never canonicalize to `wrenchrelay.app` until TLS/ownership/routing are fixed.
- Do not change DNS, connect a domain, force redirect from the existing Automotive site, or claim Search Console verified without fresh evidence.
- Keep meta titles unique by page; self-canonical on AppDeploy-hosted public information pages is acceptable as an interim identity. After verified custom-domain release, update the canonical and sitemap *in that same approved release*.
- Suggested query clusters (targets, not ranked keywords): industrial maintenance shift handoff; CMMS-ready work order draft; maintenance notes; equipment downtime documentation; independent auto shop missed call intake.
- Use clear product differentiation and short text-only structured data; do not insert fabricated reviews, FAQs as search rich-result promises, partner logos or unsupported ratings.
- Follow webmaster controls; use indexing tools only after domain verification.
