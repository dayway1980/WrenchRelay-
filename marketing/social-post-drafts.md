## Post 2 — What AI should not do / LinkedIn
AI can help organize maintenance notes. It should not pretend a missing measurement was taken—or authorize a repair.

WrenchRelay's goal: keep observed facts distinct from assumptions, preserve the evidence gap, and leave consequential decisions to qualified people.

Our public walkthrough is a simulation, not a live CMMS connection. https://wrenchrelay-industrial-7w8r70.v2.appdeploy.ai/industrial.html
